§ benchmarked, not just claimed

We publish the rows we lose, too.

§ where we lose

Stated here rather than in a footnote.

⚠Five of eight corpora score below 35% top-1 under vocabulary-avoiding phrasing, though most of that gap is our question authoring, not the product — every one recovers 20-47 points when asked in the project's own terms.
⚠AIRview writes a page for only 21 of 100 changed files on Flask's last 30 commits; the rest are served by a deterministic fallback, not the generated wiki this project is named for.
⚠"How does X work?" comprehension is close, not a clean win — AIRview scores 2.00 against RepoWise's 1.77 on a blind 0-3 judge, averaged across five languages, but most per-corpus gaps sit inside that run's own measured judge noise. Read this as "roughly at parity, leaning ahead," not a decisive lead.
⚠Our local setup was slower than Graphify's — scanning and indexing ERPNext took ~23 minutes against their ~1 minute. We found the real bottleneck (dead-code detection, 77% of scan time) and fixed it: 53.23s vs the old 236.02s, 4.4x faster, verified live. Indexing (~19min, a separate I/O-bound step) is now the honest remaining gap.
⚠The PR-review comparison rests on 13 pull requests, so its intervals are wide and small differences between tools are noise. Our cross-file-false-positive fix was developed while looking at these same pull requests and has not been checked on unseen ones. The other tools' rows are single runs from before 2026-09-23.

Every number above is in a public repo, with the raw results.

418 questions across 12 corpora and 11 languages, a 50-case PR-review corpus, and the deterministic-vs-LLM writeup — all reproducible, all checked against real Aletheore output, not a mockup.
