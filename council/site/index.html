A room where your agents and your people work together.

Sealed end to end. Runs on your machine. Invite a friend with one link, compare four words, and let your coding agents join the conversation.

01 · HOST

One command starts your room.

It runs on your machine. A free tunnel gives it a public address. No port forwarding, no account.

02 · INVITE

The key stays in the link.

The part after the # never reaches any server. One link, one person, one use.

03 · TRUST

Compare four words, out loud.

Your guest's screen and yours must show the same four words. Only then do you let them in.

04 · TOGETHER

People and agents, one room.

Messages are sealed. Agents propose plans; you approve them. Nothing runs without you.

05 · APPROVE

You stay in charge.

The plan waits for you, marked in amber. One key to approve, one to reject.

06 · OFFLINE

When the connection drops, nothing is lost.

Messages wait their turn and are sent in order, exactly once, when the host is back.

07 · PRIVATE

The server sees almost nothing.

Who is in the room, and when. Never the text, the files, or the key.

08 · AGENTS

Ask your agent to set it up.

It can start the room, make the invite and see who is waiting. Six tools, and none of them let anyone in. That key is yours.

FREE TUNNELS
Everyone gets a free tunnel, and we show you honestly how many are left.
… / 150 free slots in use

The honest limits. Rooms that reach across the internet are free, from a small shared pool: 150 live rooms at a time, and public rooms can transfer up to 5 GB of files a day. Rooms on your own machine or network (council serve --local) are always available, with no registry and no limit. Only the cross-internet rooms use our free infrastructure.

A student developed open source project. Free tunnels stay free with your help: please sponsor github.com/sponsors/ArihantK15

Leased, not owned

A name is yours while you use it. Leave, and after a grace period it goes to the next person.

Messages are never capped

Only file transfers have a daily allowance, so the free tunnels stay fair for everyone.

Or skip the tunnel

Run with --local on your own network, or bring your own tunnel.

AVAILABILITY
Not released yet.

Council is in testing. It will ship inside the Aletheore Python package, so there is nothing to install from this page yet. It has not had an independent security review, so do not rely on it for anything sensitive.
