Terms

Last updated: November 2026

Council is open source software for a shared room where people and their coding agents work together, plus an optional free tunnel service we run. These are the plain terms for both.

Council is in testing and has not had an independent security review yet. Do not rely on it for anything sensitive until it has.

Who these terms cover

These terms are for Council and its free tunnel service. The Aletheore package, GitHub App and paid plans have their own terms of service, which apply when you use them.

The software

You get Council under the licence that comes with the Aletheore package. It runs on your machine, and it is provided as it is, with no promise that it works, is secure, or fits your purpose. You are responsible for how you use it and for what your agents do in your rooms.

The free tunnel service

  • It is free, shared and best effort. There are 150 tunnels, one per live room that reaches across the internet. When they are all taken, a host waits in a queue. A host holds a tunnel only while using it, and it can be taken back after the lease ends.
  • It can change or stop. We may change the limits, move it, or end it. Rooms on your own machine or network never need it, and keep working.
  • It has limits. Messages are not capped. File transfer in a public room is limited to 5 GB a day, so the tunnels stay fair for everyone. These numbers may change.
  • It is for chat, plans and small files, not for video, bulk file hosting or serving websites.

What you must not do

  • Use Council or the tunnels to harm other people, to send spam or malware, or for anything illegal.
  • Try to break, overload or get around the service, its limits or its security.
  • Use a tunnel for something it is not for (see above).

We can block a device from the free tunnels. We cannot read a sealed room, so we do not moderate what is said in one. That, and who you let in, is the host's responsibility.

Your room, your keys

A host's device key is the root of trust for their rooms. If it is lost, the room cannot be recovered, and we cannot recover it for you. The software can save the key encrypted with a passphrase; back it up.

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss or damage from using Council or the free tunnels, including lost rooms, lost messages or downtime. Nothing here limits a right you have by law that cannot be limited.

Ending

You can stop using Council or the free tunnels at any time; release your tunnel or just stop using it. We can end or limit your access to the free tunnels, for example if you break the rules above, and we may end the service (see above). Deleting your data is described on the privacy page.

If you find a security problem in Council, please tell us privately before you publish it. See how to report one.
