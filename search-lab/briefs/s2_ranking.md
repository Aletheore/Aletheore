## Step 2: rankers (`lab_rankers.py`)
Pure Python, stdlib only, deterministic, offline. Baseline = the current ranker (import it). Then build a BM25F-style ranker over fields
with separate weights: name, title, description, intents, headings of the body (lines starting with #), first 1,500
characters of the body, category. Include: IDF, length normalisation, a bonus for covering a larger share of the query terms
(coverage), a phrase/bigram bonus for adjacent query words found in a field, prefix matching for tokens of 5+ letters,
and an improved stemmer (plurals, -ing/-ed/-tion/-ment/-ize/-ise/-ly folding) applied to both sides. Also try small
synonym folds (e.g. "bug"/"defect"/"error", "slow"/"performance"/"latency", "deploy"/"release"/"ship") only if they help on DEV.
Tune weights by coordinate ascent on DEV only (maximise 0.5*recall@1 + 0.3*recall@5 + 0.2*MRR). Then report TEST and HELD-OUT
once for the final configuration, next to the baseline. Be sceptical: if a change helps DEV but not TEST, drop it.
Constraints: one search call under 30 ms average over 1,124 skills; the cold index load under 0.5 s; the extra index data under 6 MB.
