Apache Commons Lang's `BitField.getValue()` returned incorrect (sign-extended) results for bit fields that occupy the top bit of their holder - a classic `>>` vs `>>>` bug in Java. Any caller extracting a top-bit-aligned field, such as the high byte of a 32-bit value, got a large negative number instead of the intended unsigned value. The fix switches both the `int` and `long` overloads to the unsigned right-shift operator.
